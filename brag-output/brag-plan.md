# Wavezly Brag Plan: 5 High-Converting Launch Animations

## Planning Rubric: Wavezly ("The Amazon of 3D Printing")

1. **Product Name & Actual Function:**
   Wavezly is the premier on-demand 3D printing consumer marketplace and parametric manufacturing engine. It combines an Amazon-style multi-department storefront (Trending 3D Toys, Kitchen, Home Decor, Desk Organization, EDC) with a real-time parametric 3D customizer that lets customers dial in millimeter-precise dimensions and receive custom 3D-printed products manufactured in the USA within 48 hours.

2. **Target Audience:**
   Tech enthusiasts, desk setup collectors, homeowners craving custom drawer organization, gadget lovers, and TikTok/X creators who want trending 3D-printed toys, articulated fidgets, and precision-fit organizers without needing their own 3D printer.

3. **Strongest Claim / Most Absurd Reality:**
   "Over 200,000 custom organizer variations, zero CAD skills needed, manufactured in our Boise, Idaho print studio and shipped to your door in 48 hours with a 100% Fit Guarantee."

4. **Visual Identity:**
   - Brand Dark / Background: `#131921` (Amazon dark slate / navy)
   - Secondary Dark: `#232f3e`
   - Accent Primary: `#ff9900` (Wavezly amber smile orange)
   - Accent Highlight: `#ffd814` (Wavezly prime yellow)
   - Surface Light: `#fafafc` / `#ffffff`
   - Border / Precision Gray: `#374151` / `#d5d9d9`
   - Typography: Inter, Impact, system-ui

5. **Grounded Verification Checklist:**
   - Boise, Idaho 3D Print Farm Cluster (referenced in `ManufacturingSpecs.tsx`, `PrintFarmClusterModal.tsx`, `HomePage.tsx`)
   - 100% Fit Guarantee & Millimeter Precision Customizer (in `ConsumerSizingWizard.tsx`, `CustomizerPage.tsx`)
